# Rules & Judging — NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026

Event: 13–14 Nov 2026, Sewedy University of Technology (SUT). Source: docs/Judging_Guidelines_Cairo_2026.pdf

## 5 judging criteria -> how our project answers each
| Criterion | What judges want | Our answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | Real problem; who benefits and how | Astronaut safety + EVA efficiency on first crewed Mars missions; also mission planners & rover teams today |
| **Creativity** | Original idea / fresh approach | A *generator* (any site), dual Plan (tablet) + Walk (HUD) modes, ice/water layer for "survival" |
| **Validity** | Sound science, works, evidence/testing/demo | Validation on Jezero: our route vs. Perseverance's real traverse + raw images; slope/hazard from real DTMs |
| **Relevance** | Meet challenge objectives; use NASA data meaningfully and show how it contributes | All challenge objectives covered (≥3 data types, safety, route); all 5 provided resources used; a "data -> decision" panel showing each layer's role |
| **Presentation** | Problem, solution, method, results, clear visuals | Story-driven demo video + slides: astronaut scenario -> method -> validation results -> impact |
